## [0.1.0](https://github.com/StepKie/StudyHub/releases/tag/0.1.0) — 2026-11-11

### New Features

- **Flashcard trainer** — study decks with spaced repetition and Anki's four answer buttons, each showing when the card comes back.
- **Import cards** — load an Anki "Notes in Plain Text" export or a CSV / TSV file.
- **Study by section** — an imported deck's sections (its tags) are listed on the deck, each with its own due and new counts and its own Study button.
- **Study more** — when the daily limit of 20 new cards is reached, continue with 20 more.
- **History and streak** — a History page shows the last 14 days of reviews per deck, and the deck card shows today's count and your streak.
- **Backup and restore** — save all decks and progress to a file and restore them on another device.
- **Install as an app** — add to the Home Screen on iPhone, Android or desktop; works offline.
